Veetu Kanaku privacy policy

Boss Infotech. Effective 11 September 2026.

Veetu Kanaku is an expense, income and investment tracker for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Signing in is optional

Veetu Kanaku works fully without an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication (a Google service) receives your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture so your cloud backup can be identified. Boss Infotech does not receive your Google password.

Cloud backup

When you are signed in, your entries can be backed up to Google Cloud Firestore under your account ID, either when you tap Back up now or automatically when you leave the app. Only you can read or write that copy. It is deleted when you delete your account, either in the app under More or by emailing us. If you never sign in, nothing is sent to the cloud.

Your entries stay on your phone

Expenses, income, investments, debts, current values and notes you enter are stored on your device. They are sent nowhere else unless you sign in and use cloud backup, or share a backup file yourself. Boss Infotech does not sell this information, and Veetu Kanaku does not connect to your bank, broker or any financial account.

Backups and files

When you use Back up or Export, Veetu Kanaku creates a file and opens Android's share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it, such as Google Drive or email. If Android's device backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include Veetu Kanaku's data in that backup, which is managed by Google under your Google account settings.

Advertising

Veetu Kanaku shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, approximate location based on IP address, app interaction and diagnostic information. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Your expense and investment entries are never shared with advertisers.

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area and the UK, Veetu Kanaku asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings.

Purchases

The optional Remove ads purchase is handled by Google Play under Google's terms. Veetu Kanaku does not see your payment details. The app stores only whether ads have been removed and checks this with Google Play.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Veetu Kanaku schedules a notification on your phone. No information is sent anywhere to do this. You can turn it off in More or in Android settings.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and cloud backup, open More and tap Delete account, or follow the steps at our account deletion page. To remove entries from this phone, open Veetu Kanaku, go to More, and choose Delete all data. Uninstalling the app also removes its data from your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere are controlled by you.

Children

Veetu Kanaku is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Not financial advice

Veetu Kanaku records numbers you enter. It does not provide investment, tax or financial advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
