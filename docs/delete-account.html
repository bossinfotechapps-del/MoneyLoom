Delete your MoneyLoom account

App: MoneyLoom (com.bossinfotech.moneyloom), Boss Infotech

Delete it in the app

  1. Open MoneyLoom.
  2. Go to More.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This unlinks your Google account and deletes your cloud backup straight away.

Delete it by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email bossinfotech.apps@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my MoneyLoom account". Requests are completed within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Deleted immediately: your account record and your cloud backup (expenses, income, investments, debts and settings stored in it).
  • Kept on your phone: entries stored on the device. Remove those with More → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Kept for legal reasons: none. MoneyLoom keeps no other personal data. Purchase records are held by Google Play under Google's policies.
