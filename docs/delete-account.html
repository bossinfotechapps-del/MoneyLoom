Delete your MoneyLoom account

MoneyLoom · Boss Infotech · Android package: com.bossinfotech.moneyloom

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open MoneyLoom.
  2. Sign in with the Google account you use for MoneyLoom cloud backup, if prompted.
  3. Go to More.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm your request.

The in-app deletion process removes the MoneyLoom cloud backup and its saved backup versions associated with your account, then removes the MoneyLoom account link used for Google sign-in and cloud backup.

Request deletion without the app

You do not need a GitHub account or the MoneyLoom app installed to request account deletion.

Email bossinfotech.apps@gmail.com from the Google email address you used with MoneyLoom. Use the subject MoneyLoom account deletion. We will use your email address to identify the relevant MoneyLoom account and process your request.

Account-deletion requests submitted by email are processed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Cloud backup: the MoneyLoom cloud backup associated with your account, including its saved backup versions.
  • MoneyLoom account link: the account used by MoneyLoom for Google sign-in and cloud backup.

What is not deleted automatically

  • Data stored only on your phone: use More → Delete all data in MoneyLoom, or remove the app and its local data from your device.
  • Backup or export files you saved or shared yourself: delete these separately from the locations where you saved them.
  • Your Google account: deleting your MoneyLoom account does not delete your Google account.
  • Google Play purchase records: these are managed by Google Play under Google's applicable policies.

Contact
